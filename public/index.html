Take part in research at the Behavioural Research Lab

The Department of Economics runs studies on how people decide: about money, risk, trust, information and each other. Sessions are short, they are paid, and they run on campus. Register once to join the pool, then pick a session time for any study you are eligible for.

The rules of the pool

What the lab asks of you

Once Each person may take a given study one time only
30 days Minimum wait between two different studies
2 no-shows Unexplained absences before your membership is paused

The waiting period exists because somebody who played one decision task last week arrives at the next one already knowing its shape, and that changes the result. If you cannot make a session, cancel it on this site and it does not count against you.
